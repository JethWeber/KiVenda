# KiVenda - Product Requirements Document

## Overview
Desktop software for small/medium Angolan merchants (Cantinas, Mini Markets, etc.).
Focus: Speed, simplicity, professional look. Avoid "ERP 2000s" clutter.

## Design Language: Weber Design System
- Primary: Green #16A34A
- Secondary: Blue #1E3A8A
- Style: Fluent Design, Windows 11, Glassmorphism, 12px corners.

## Planned Screens

1. **Dashboard**
   - KPIs: Today's Sales, Cash Balance, Estimated Profit, Low Stock, Sales Count.
   - Modern Chart (Sales Trends).
   - Recent Sales Table.
   - Alerts: Low Stock, Open Cashier, Pending Backup.

2. **Sales (POS)**
   - High-priority UI.
   - Left: Product search, Barcode input, categories.
   - Right: Cart, Quantity, Discount, Total.
   - Large "Receive" and "Cancel" buttons.
   - Barcode scanner indicator.

3. **Products (Inventory)**
   - Modern Table: Code, Barcode, Name, Category, Buy/Sell Price, Stock, State.
   - Actions: New Product, Edit, Stock Entry.

4. **Cashier (Caixa)**
   - Status: Open/Closed.
   - Current Balance, In/Out flow history.

5. **Reports**
   - Analytical view: Sales by product, Profitability, Export to PDF/Excel.

6. **Settings**
   - Company info, Scanner toggle ("Habilitar Scanner de Código de Barras"), Backup, Theme.

## Target Experience
"Fewer clicks, faster actions." Premium product feeling.
